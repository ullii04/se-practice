### US-01
**Story:** As a Student, I want to view room availability, so that I can find a free room and suitable time for study.  
**Priority:** High  
**Assumption:** Room availability information is current.

### US-02
**Story:** As a Student, I want to book a free room for a time slot, so that I can reserve a place for individual or group study.  
**Priority:** High  
**Assumption:** The selected room and time slot are available when the booking is requested.

### US-03
**Story:** As a Student, I want to cancel a booking I made, so that the room becomes available when I no longer need it.  
**Priority:** High  
**Assumption:** A student can cancel only a booking they made.

### US-04
**Story:** As a Student, I want to receive confirmation after I book or cancel a room, so that I know the action was completed successfully.  
**Priority:** Medium  
**Assumption:** Confirmation is provided only for a booking or cancellation as defined by UC-06.

### US-05
**Story:** As an Administrator, I want to block or unblock a room, so that I can control whether the room is available for booking.  
**Priority:** High  
**Assumption:** A blocked room cannot be booked, and unblocking returns it to normal availability.

### US-06
**Story:** As an Administrator, I want to review room usage over a period, so that I can understand how the study rooms are being used.  
**Priority:** Medium  
**Assumption:** Usage information is based on room bookings for the selected period.
